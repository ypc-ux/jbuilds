## Questions Before You Start?

**Q: Should I customize the email?**
A: YES. Every email should reference something specific about their product or announcement.

**Q: Should I send to everyone at once?**
A: No. Send 5, wait for responses, then send 5 more. Batch emails look spammy.

**Q: What if they say no?**
A: Good. Move to next person. Takes 10-15 "no's" to get a "yes" in founder space. Normal.

**Q: What if nobody responds?**
A: Wait 48 hours, send 1-line follow-up: "Wanted to make sure this didn't get buried. Would love to chat - repo links in first email."

**Q: What if I get offered employee role instead of co-founder?**
A: Negotiate. "Can we do 0.5-1% equity on co-founder track instead?" If they say no, politely pass.
